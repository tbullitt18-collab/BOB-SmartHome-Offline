## 1. The High-Density Housing Outage Dilemma
When an apartment complex or senior living facility suffers power and internet failure:
- **Smart Locks Fail Closed or Open:** Cloud-managed locks become unmanageable. Residents are trapped inside units or locked out of exterior gates.
- **Emergency Battery Exhaustion:** Standby generator or UPS batteries drain in 3–5 hours because common areas and unoccupied apartments continue pulling parasitic load.
- **Property Damage:** Freezing pipe bursts or HVAC overheating occur without maintenance awareness.
- **Severe Legal & Insurance Liability:** Failure to provide egress lighting, emergency lock release, and verifiable maintenance records during a storm exposes owners to wrongful death or tenant negligence lawsuits.
